# Monica Brand and Ecosystem Marks

Monica uses two visually and contractually distinct package identities.

## Official Monica mark

The purple Monica logo and `Monica.*` NuGet package IDs identify packages published and maintained by the Monica project. Independent publishers must not reuse, recolor, modify, or present that mark as their package icon, and must not use a `Monica.*` package ID.

## Monica Compatibility Mark

Independent publishers may use the emerald Monica Compatibility Mark shipped with `$monica-third-party-module-development` when their package follows Monica Ecosystem Standard v1. Publishers may also use their own icon.

The mark is a self-attested compatibility indicator. It does not mean that Monica reviewed, certified, verified, endorsed, supports, or warrants the package.

When using the mark, include this notice in the package README:

> This community package is independently maintained and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by the Monica project.

Do not use “official”, “certified”, or “verified” in connection with an independent package. Do not combine the compatibility mark with another logo in a way that implies common ownership.

## Software licensing

This brand policy is separate from Monica's MIT software license. The MIT license governs Monica source code; it does not grant permission to imply project endorsement. Third-party package authors remain free to select open-source or proprietary terms for their own work, subject to applicable third-party notices and licenses.

This policy is practical project guidance rather than legal advice. Obtain legal review before relying on it for a formal trademark program or a disputed use.
