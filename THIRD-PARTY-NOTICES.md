## Embedded Fonts

The Monica Guide wizard (`Monica.Guide.App/wwwroot/fonts`) embeds latin-subset WOFF2 builds of three typeface families so the offline setup UI matches the Monica.Docs design language without any network access:

- Bricolage Grotesque (variable weight) — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bricolage+Grotesque
- IBM Plex Sans — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans
- IBM Plex Mono — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono

All three are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the subsets are redistributed unmodified under its terms.
