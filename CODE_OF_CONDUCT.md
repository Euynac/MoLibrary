# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Monica to be a practical, respectful, and technical open-source community. Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate professionally and focus on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume technical disagreement is normal.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Keep discussions focused on Monica and its ecosystem.
- Respect maintainers' time and project scope.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained off-topic disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Bad-faith issue spam or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; or restrict participation when behavior harms the project.

For serious conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately through the repository owner channels.
